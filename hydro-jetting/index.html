Hydro Jetting in Windsor, CO

For drains that clog again after a cable, lines with roots, and kitchen lines coated in grease. A camera look comes first, and the price is explained before work starts.

  • Roots and grease
  • Camera inspection first

Symptoms

A drain that clogs again a few weeks after it was cleared

Most people reach this page after a cable machine opened the drain and it clogged again within weeks, or after a cable caught in the line. Both point to the same thing. The blockage is not a lump of debris but a coating or a root mass that a cable only pokes a hole through.

Hydro jetting clears that kind of blockage. A hose with a nozzle sends high-pressure water in several directions, scouring the pipe wall instead of just opening a channel. Sewer jetters can run at up to about 4,000 psi, and the volume of water is what carries the debris out.

Comparison

Cable machine or hydro jetting: which one fits

Common drain situations and how a cable machine and hydro jetting handle each

Roots through a jointCuts a channel, and the roots regrowCuts roots and flushes debris, but the entry point remains
Grease coating the pipe wallPokes a hole, and the grease re-formsScours the wall clean

Roots that return are still a repair question. Jetting buys time, and sewer line repair removes the entry point.

Process

What happens on a hydro jetting visit

  1. 1

    Camera inspection

    A camera shows the blockage and confirms the pipe is sound enough for jetting.

  2. 2

    Access the line

    The jetter connects at a cleanout, so it helps to know where yours is.

  3. 3

    Jet toward the clog

    The nozzle is fed in with water pushing debris ahead of it.

  4. 4

    Cut and rinse

    A spinning nozzle cuts roots and scours the wall on the way back.

  5. 5

    Camera again

    A second pass confirms the line is clean and shows any damage the jetting uncovered.

Jetting needs a cleanout or another accessible opening. The International Plumbing Code (IPC) calls for cleanouts in building sewers, so most homes have one. If none exists, one can be added, and that is quoted separately.

Windsor

Where hydro jetting earns its cost in Windsor

Restaurants and food service along the Main Street corridor are the obvious grease cases, and kitchen lines in houses collect the same buildup more slowly. Lines with roots are the other case, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees.

The Town requires service providers to notify it when work is done in the sanitary sewer system, including plugged lines and debris removal. That applies to jetting as well, so ask how the notice is handled.

Newer subdivisions rarely have roots in the line yet. Repeat clogs there can come from grease or from a sag left by construction, and the camera shows which.

Limits

When jetting is the wrong tool

Jetting cleans pipe. It does not fix pipe. A cracked or collapsed section, a bellied line that holds water, or old Orangeburg pipe (bituminized fiber, found in many homes built from the 1940s to the early 1970s) can be damaged by high pressure. That is why the camera look comes first.

In those cases the answer is repair or, for a line that is failing along its length, sewer line replacement. A camera look after the jetting pass shows which of the two applies.
