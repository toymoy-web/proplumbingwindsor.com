The area

Old Town Windsor and the streets around Main Street

Old Town is the historic center of Windsor, the blocks around the two-block downtown and Main Street. The town was platted in 1882 and incorporated in 1890, so these are the oldest streets in town.

Windsor Lake and Boardwalk Park sit right beside this part of town. No official boundary is published for Old Town, so we use Main Street, Walnut Street and the lake as anchors and do not claim exact edges.

Housing

What the older housing looks like

One real estate profile of the downtown area gives a median build year of 1919, with century-old ranch-style homes and bungalows on small lots, many with fish-scale siding. That is one source and not a survey of every house, so a given address can differ.

The Town has done its own historic work here. A 1997 survey covered buildings built before 1947, and a residential survey of select homes on Walnut Street was completed in 2018 and 2019.

Practical guidance

Plumbing guidance for older homes with mature yards

Mature trees are common on older residential streets, and tree roots are a known cause of sewer line trouble in any older neighborhood. If a drain keeps slowing after it has been cleared, a camera look shows whether roots or a damaged section are the reason.

Water in Windsor comes from more than one source. The Town's own information says roughly 30 percent comes from Greeley and about 70 percent from Fort Collins-Loveland and North Weld sources, so ask your provider about the water at your address.

Around town

Landmarks we pass in Old Town

Old Town sits near Boardwalk Park on Windsor Lake, Main Park southeast of downtown and Chimney Park, which includes former Great Western Sugar factory property. The Windsor History Museum at 100 N 5th Street holds the original 1882 train depot.

Windsor is a wider town than its center, and newer areas such as Water Valley and RainDance have their own pages. Neighbors in Timnath reach the same line.
