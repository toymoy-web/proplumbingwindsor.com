Plumbing Services in Windsor, CO

Emergency plumbing at any hour, plus the everyday work that keeps a home or business running. Each service has its own page with the symptoms, the options and the cost factors.

Water heaters and fixtures

Water heaters, faucets, toilets and more

A tank that leaks or runs cold goes to water heater repair, and a unit past its life goes to water heater installation and replacement. Households that want continuous hot water can read about tankless water heater service.

Inside the house, dripping and weak faucets are covered under faucet and sink repair, running and leaking toilets under toilet repair and installation, and jammed disposals under garbage disposal repair and installation. Tub and shower projects, including valve and drain work, are on the page for shower and bathtub installation.

Drains and sewer

Clogs, backups and the lines behind them

A backing-up drain is an emergency when it is the main line, and drain cleaning is the first call. A drain that keeps clogging after a cable may need hydro jetting, and a camera inspection decides whether the line needs sewer line repair or sewer line replacement.

Basements bring their own pumps. A failed sump pump in a storm is a job for sump pump installation and repair, and the drains, ejectors and bathrooms below grade are covered under basement plumbing.

Leaks and pipes

Finding leaks and fixing pipes

A meter that moves with everything off is the cue for leak detection, and a warm spot in a concrete floor points toward slab leak detection and repair. Pipe that has let go is handled as burst pipe repair, and winter freezes fall under frozen pipe repair.

Worn supply piping is replaced under repiping, and the line from the meter to the house is covered under water line repair and replacement. Both start with a look at where the pipe is failing.

Water damage

When water has already reached the house

Extraction and drying after a burst pipe, a sewer backup or a failed pump are covered under water damage restoration. The first 24 to 48 hours of containment and drying are covered on the page for water mitigation. Both have their own dedicated phone line, listed on those pages, because they are a different service from plumbing repair.

Systems and commercial

Gas, water quality, backflow and business work

New appliance hookups and repairs to gas piping are covered under gas line installation and repair. Water quality has two separate answers: water softener installation and repair for hardness, and water filtration installation for taste, chlorine and lead concerns.

Irrigation systems and businesses on Town water need yearly testing, covered under backflow testing and prevention. Offices, shops and restaurants can start with commercial plumbing.
