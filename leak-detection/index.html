Leak Detection in Windsor, CO

A meter that keeps turning, a bill that jumped, or a warm patch on the floor all mean water is escaping. Hidden leaks are located first and opened up only where needed.

  • Acoustic and thermal tools
  • Meter and pressure tests
  • Minimal demolition
  • Price before work starts
Symptoms

A bill that jumped, a meter that will not stop, a stain with no obvious source

Most hidden leaks announce themselves indirectly. The water bill rises with no change in habits, the meter dial moves while every fixture is off, a floor feels warm, a wall sounds hollow, or a musty smell appears in a room with no visible water. Water pressure that drops without explanation is another clue.

Leak detection is the work of finding where the water is going before anyone cuts into drywall or concrete. It combines tests that confirm a leak exists with tools that pinpoint it, so the repair, covered under burst pipe repair or a smaller fix, targets one spot instead of a whole wall.

Method

How a leak gets located, step by step

Leak detection tools, what each does and when it is used
Tool or testWhat it doesBest for
Meter testShows whether water moves through the meter with every fixture offConfirming a leak exists
Pressure testIsolates the supply line and watches for a pressure dropTelling a supply leak from a drain leak
Acoustic listeningMicrophones hear the hiss of pressurized water through pipe and floorPinpointing a supply leak in a slab or wall
Thermal imagingA camera shows temperature differences from wet or hot-water-affected areasHot water leaks and wet insulation
Moisture meterReads moisture in drywall, wood and flooringMapping how far water has spread
Tracer gasA harmless gas mix is pushed into the line and sniffed at the surfaceLeaks that sound and thermal tools cannot settle

No single tool settles every case. Detection usually starts with the meter and pressure tests, then adds acoustic and thermal tools until the location is clear. The pressure test follows the same idea the International Plumbing Code (IPC) uses to prove new water piping: hold working pressure, or 50 psi, for 15 minutes and watch the gauge.

On site

What happens on a leak detection visit

Detection ends with information. What happens next is your decision.

  1. 1

    Talk through the symptoms

    Bill history, damp spots, sounds and recent plumbing work narrow where to start.

  2. 2

    Confirm the leak

    A meter test and a pressure test show whether water is escaping and whether it is on the supply or the drain side.

  3. 3

    Narrow the location

    Acoustic listening, thermal imaging and a moisture meter reduce the search to a small area.

  4. 4

    Mark and explain

    The leak location is marked, and the options are explained with an honest view of the repair and any restoration.

  5. 5

    Quote the repair

    The fix is quoted separately, so finding the leak never commits you to the repair.

Do it first

Checks that rule out the easy leaks before anyone is called

  • ✓Read the meter, wait 30 minutes with no water in use, and read it again.
  • ✓Look for a leak indicator on the meter, often a small triangle or dial that spins at low flow.
  • ✓Add a few drops of food coloring to each toilet tank, wait 15 minutes, and check the bowl for color.
  • ✓Check hose bibs, irrigation zones and the water heater relief valve discharge for steady dripping.
  • ✓Turn off the main shutoff. If the meter stops, the leak is inside the house, and if it keeps moving, it is on the line before the shutoff.
Windsor

What a Windsor water bill can and cannot tell you

The Town of Windsor bills a base fee plus tiered consumption, and its water, sewer and stormwater rates rose on February 1, 2026 to fund wastewater and water line projects. A higher bill this year is not proof of a leak, so compare gallons used and not dollars, and check the usage line against last year's same month.

Which provider bills the house also matters. The Town serves Weld County addresses, Fort Collins-Loveland Water District serves Larimer County addresses, and North Weld County Water District serves Harmony Ridge and Alexander Estates. Each can tell you your usage pattern, and a steady overnight draw on that record points to a leak.

The meter is the dividing line. A leak between the meter and the house is on your side, and a leak in the street is the provider's, which is why the test with the main shutoff closed is worth doing. A confirmed line leak is a water line repair or replacement job.

A leak report is only useful if someone can act on it. It names the location, the depth, the pipe material and the likely cause, with photographs and the meter readings from before and after the test. A repair crew or an insurer can use that record, and it keeps the opening in a wall or slab as small as it can be.

Not every high reading is a leaking pipe. A running toilet, a stuck irrigation zone or a failed relief valve can all keep a meter turning, so those get ruled out first. Finding one of them early spares you the cost of tracing a leak that is not there, and it keeps repiping off the table until the pipe itself is shown to be the problem.

Leak checks are common in neighborhoods across Windsor, including Water Valley, RainDance and the older streets near Main Street. A reading at the meter with every fixture off is the first step, and it works the same in any of them.

Pricing

What affects the price of leak detection

Detection is priced by scope. A quick meter and pressure test costs less than full acoustic and thermal work on a large home, and the price is explained before work starts.

Size of the homeMore pipe and more floor area mean more time.
AccessFinished basements and slabs take longer to survey.
Number of testsA leak that shows on the first test ends the work sooner.
Repair afterwardFinding the leak and fixing it are quoted separately.
Time of callAfter-hours calls can price differently.

Homeowner policies commonly cover water damage from a sudden leak and sometimes the cost of finding it, but not the pipe itself or long-term seepage. Ask your agent whether detection is covered before you file.
