What Eaton plumbing calls look like

Benjamin Eaton platted this railroad stop in 1882 and gave the blocks tree names, so downtown follows Elm. Incorporation came in 1892, and the 2020 census counted 5,802 people amid irrigated farmland.

Calls split along that line. Houses by the rail corridor lean on decades-old plumbing, so drain cleaning and leak detection top the list. Farmsteads on the outskirts bring questions about wells, septic tanks and frost. Emergency dispatch is available to Eaton around the clock.

While you wait

Burst Pipe or Active Leak in Eaton? Before We Arrive, Do This

Eaton homes range from the old streets near Elm Avenue to rural lots out in the fields. The first four steps are the same for all of them.

1

Close the main shutoff

In an older in-town home the valve is often on a basement or crawl space wall near the front. On a rural lot it may sit in a pit or a utility room. Turn it until the water stops.

2

Kill power to wet areas

If water is touching outlets or the panel, stay out and cut power only from a dry spot. Otherwise switch off the breakers for the wet rooms and the water heater.

3

Clear the area

Lift rugs, electronics and stored boxes off wet floors, and keep children and pets away if sewage is involved. Photograph the damage first for your insurer.

4

Call the 24/7 line

Call (970) 708-6013, give the street and the ZIP, 80615, and say what the water is doing. The line is answered around the clock.
