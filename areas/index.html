Plumbing Service Areas Near Windsor, CO

One 24/7 line covers Windsor and the towns around it. Each town has its own utilities, housing and plumbing quirks, so each has its own page.

Home base

Windsor and the surrounding towns

Windsor, ZIP 80550, sits in both Weld and Larimer counties, and its home page covers the town itself. Beyond it, the same line serves six nearby towns, each with different housing and utilities.

Severance, about 7 miles north, is covered on its own page, with ZIP 80550 for most homes and 80546 for PO boxes, and a mix of an older core and fast-growing subdivisions. Timnath, between Fort Collins and Windsor, has its page for ZIP 80547, where growth has been among the fastest in Colorado.

Farther out

Johnstown, Milliken, Greeley and Eaton

Johnstown, ZIP 80534, sits between the Big Thompson and Little Thompson rivers, and its page covers flood-season basement calls. Milliken, ZIP 80543, has its page built around housing from the 1960s and 1970s. Greeley, ZIP 80631, is the largest city in the area, and its page covers older homes and commercial work.

ZIP codes overlap at town edges. A Milliken address can carry 80534, 80634 or 80651, and a Severance address can carry 80550, 80615 or 80610. A call from any of them reaches the same line.

Different rules

Why each town gets its own page

Every town has its own utility, its own permit office and its own rules. Windsor requires a building permit to install or move plumbing, Severance runs its own water and sewer utilities, and Greeley has an active cross-connection program. A plumber who treats them as one place misses those differences.

If you are not sure which provider serves you, your water bill names it, and the page for your town explains who to call for a main line problem. The same 24/7 line answers calls from every town listed on this page.
