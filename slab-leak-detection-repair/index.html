Symptoms

Signs of a leak under a concrete floor

A warm patch on the floor, the sound of running water with all fixtures off, damp carpet along a wall, cracks that appear with moisture, or a water bill that rises for no reason are the usual signs. Hot water lines under a slab can make a floor warm to the touch.

A slab leak is a break in a water line that runs in or under concrete. Slab leak detection finds the break without removing the whole floor, and slab leak repair then fixes the line by opening a small section, rerouting the line, or lining it, depending on what the footage and location show.

Options

How a slab leak is repaired

The honest choice depends on the number of breaks. One leak is usually a spot repair. A line with several pinhole leaks often points toward a reroute or repiping.

  1. 1

    Locate the break

    Acoustic listening, thermal imaging and a pressure test, the same kind of test the International Plumbing Code (IPC) uses on new piping, narrow the leak to a small area.

  2. 2

    Choose the repair

    A spot repair cuts a small opening in the slab. A reroute abandons the leaking line and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. Lining restores a sound line from the inside.

  3. 3

    Open and fix

    The concrete is cut along the located line, the pipe is repaired or replaced, and the area is tested.

  4. 4

    Restore

    Concrete is patched and flooring restored, and the water-damage side is checked and dried if needed.

Windsor

Slab realities in Windsor

Windsor sits on the Front Range plains along the Cache la Poudre River. The Colorado Geological Survey identifies expansive soil as a major geologic hazard in the state, and shifting clay can stress water lines that run through or under a slab. Whether a particular lot is affected is a question for the footage and the repair history, not a given.

Slab-level plumbing in Windsor is more often in garages, basements and additions than under whole main floors, because basements are common. That shapes the search, and it makes basement floor drains and sump systems part of the picture. Sump pump installation and repair is the first stop when the cause is groundwater and not a pipe.

While you wait

What to do while you wait for a slab leak visit

  • ✓Close the main shutoff if the meter races or water is pooling.
  • ✓Stay out of any area where water touches outlets or cords.
  • ✓Move rugs, boxes and stored items off a damp slab.
  • ✓Note where the floor is warm or damp, and photograph it.
  • ✓Avoid drilling or cutting the slab yourself, since you may hit another line.
