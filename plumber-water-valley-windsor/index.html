The area

Water Valley, the lake community in south Windsor

Water Valley is a master-planned community in south Windsor, built around lakes and water features between the lakes and the Cache la Poudre River. Former gravel mines along the river are now lakes bordered by housing and a golf course.

The community includes 27 holes of golf at Pelican Lakes. Trumark Homes opened Pelican Shores at Water Valley as its first Colorado community, and commercial development runs along State Highway 257 nearby.

Housing

What kind of homes are here

Local agents describe a mix of older resale homes and newer construction with mature landscaping, and many homes back up to water or open space. Build years are not published for the community as a whole, so the age of a given home varies.

Some sections have HOA dues and rules, and which sections are affected is something the HOA documents settle. Check them before adding visible equipment or changing exterior plumbing.

Practical guidance

Plumbing guidance for lakeside and open-space homes

Before the first hard freeze, disconnect hoses and shut off exterior spigots. A line that freezes and splits can run unseen until it thaws, and an exposed hose bib is one of the first places it happens.

If the yard turns soft or the water bill jumps, test the meter with every fixture off. A moving meter points to a leak somewhere between the meter and the fixtures, including irrigation lines.
