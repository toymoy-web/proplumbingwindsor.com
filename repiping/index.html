Repiping & Whole-Home Pipe Replacement in Windsor, CO

For homes with pinhole leaks, rusty water, falling pressure or pipe material that is past its life. The old supply piping is replaced, with a written scope before the first wall is opened.

Symptoms

Pinhole leaks, rusty water, weak pressure, or the same pipe leaking again

A second or third leak in the same system, brown or orange water when a faucet first opens, pressure that fades at fixtures, and green stains on copper are the signs that pipe is wearing out from the inside. A single leak is a repair. Repeated leaks are a pattern.

Repiping, also called whole-home pipe replacement, replaces the supply piping that carries pressurized water to fixtures. It is chosen when repair after repair costs more than a new system, or when the material itself is known to fail.

Windsor has older homes near Main Street, platted in 1882, and many homes built in the last 20 years. Most repiping calls come from the older group, and newer homes more often need a targeted repair than a full replacement.

Decision

Spot repair or full repipe: a test

A leak search first, covered under leak detection, shows whether the pipe is failing along its length or at one point. If the failing pipe is the line from the meter, the work is water line repair or replacement and not a repipe.

Repair when

  • Only one section has leaked
  • The pipe material has no known failure pattern
  • Water quality and pressure are fine elsewhere
  • The home is newer than the pipe's failure window

Repipe when

  • Leaks keep appearing in different places
  • The pipe is galvanized or polybutylene
  • Water is brown or orange at first draw
  • Repair bills are approaching the cost of new pipe

Process

What a repiping job involves

  1. 1

    Survey and scope

    The pipe routes, fixtures and access points are mapped, and a written scope is prepared.

  2. 2

    Permit

    The Town of Windsor requires a building permit to install or move plumbing, and re-piping is covered by that requirement.

  3. 3

    Install new supply lines

    New PEX or copper is run through walls, floors and the attic, usually by routing around finished surfaces where possible.

  4. 4

    Test and connect

    Each line is pressure-tested as the International Plumbing Code (IPC) requires, then connected to fixtures and the water heater.

  5. 5

    Repair openings

    Access holes in drywall are patched, and finish work is scheduled separately.

Windsor

Windsor details that affect a repipe

The Town's most recent water report lists a required service line inventory and notes that lead in drinking water comes mainly from service lines and home plumbing. In the Town's 2024 sampling, 3 of 32 homes tested above the 15 parts per billion lead action level, though the system as a whole did not exceed it. An older home with unknown piping is worth a materials check.

Water also comes from three sources. The Town buys about 30 percent of its water from the City of Greeley and about 70 percent from Fort Collins-Loveland Water District and North Weld County Water District. Water chemistry affects pipe life, so ask your provider for its latest quality report if you are choosing a material.

Before the job

What to do before a repiping crew arrives

  • ✓Clear access to the water heater, the main shutoff and the walls where pipe will run.
  • ✓Plan for the water to be off for part of each day.
  • ✓Move valuables and fragile items away from walls that will be opened.
  • ✓Confirm in writing who patches drywall and who paints.
  • ✓Ask for the permit number once it is issued.
