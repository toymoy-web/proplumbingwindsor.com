The area

RainDance, the resort community in south Windsor

RainDance is a large master-planned community in south Windsor with several phases and product types. It centers on RainDance National Resort and Golf, an 18-hole course designed by Fred Funk, and the RainDance River Resort with a pool, waterslides and a lazy river.

Residents have about 15 miles of trails and a 13-acre play park. These details come from a local real estate source, and the community is still adding phases.

Housing

A wide mix of home types

Builders include Trumark Homes and Hartford Homes, and the mix runs from condos and 50 and 60 foot wide lots to semi-custom golf-course homes in The Fairways and one-acre estates at Acadia. One listing in the Sugar Hills enclave is a semi-custom ranch built in 2022, and rentals include the Village Townhomes and Mountain View Apartments.

Most of this housing is newer, but phases differ, so the age of a given home depends on where it sits.

Practical guidance

Plumbing guidance for newer and larger homes

New builds usually come with a builder warranty, and the terms differ by builder. Read yours before calling anyone, because outside work may affect coverage.

Larger homes often have several bathrooms and a bigger hot water demand than older ones. If the heater runs out quickly, water heater repair checks the unit before anyone suggests a bigger one.

Before you call

What to have ready
