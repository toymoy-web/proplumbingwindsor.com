Urgent

A clogged drain is an emergency when it is the main line

One slow sink is an annoyance. Water or sewage coming up in the lowest drain of the house is different, because it means the main line is blocked and every fixture upstream is feeding into it. That calls for drain cleaning right away, before the next shower or load of laundry makes it worse.

Drain cleaning means clearing a blockage from a drain or sewer line with a plunger, a cable machine or high-pressure water, then confirming the line is open. The method depends on where the clog is and what it is made of.

Triage

One fixture or the main line? Read the pattern

Where the water shows up tells you how big the problem is. Use the lowest drain in the house as the guide, usually a basement floor drain or a basement tub or shower.

Methods

How a clog gets cleared, from simple to serious

Technicians match the tool to the blockage. A tool that is too light leaves the clog partly in place, and one that is too aggressive risks an old pipe.

  1. 1

    Plunger or hand auger

    Clears soft clogs in a single fixture, such as hair in a tub or paper in a toilet.

  2. 2

    Cable machine

    A motorized cable with a cutting head clears grease, roots and debris in branch and main lines.

  3. 3

    Camera inspection

    A waterproof camera on a flexible rod shows what the blockage is, how far it runs, and whether the pipe is cracked or sagging. Sewer line repair starts from that footage.

  4. 4

    Hydro jetting

    High-pressure water scours the pipe wall when a cable only opens a hole through the clog. The page on hydro jetting covers when that is the right call.

Windsor

What causes drain trouble in Windsor homes

Tree roots are a common cause of main line clogs, and Windsor's older neighborhoods have mature trees growing over lines that are decades old. Roots enter at joints and small cracks. A line that clogs repeatedly in the same spot usually has a root or a damaged joint there and not a random clog.

Grease is the other repeat offender. It cools and hardens inside the line, then catches paper and wipes. Kitchen drains and restaurant lines suffer most, which is why recurring kitchen clogs often point toward a jetting visit and not another cable.

Responsibility is worth knowing before you call. The Town's wastewater page says owners are responsible for the sewer line extending from their property, and the Town requires service providers to notify it when work is done in the sanitary sewer system, including plugged lines. Ask whoever you hire how that notice is handled.

The Town provides wastewater service in the Weld County limits of Windsor, and only storm sewer on the Larimer County side. Confirm who handles sewer for your address before assuming a main line problem is the Town's. A backup hitting several neighbors at once points toward the main, and Town Hall's number is 970-674-2400.

Right now

What to do while a drain is backing up

  • ✓Stop using water in the house, including the washer and dishwasher.
  • ✓Skip chemical drain cleaners, which can damage old pipe and leave the water hazardous to whoever clears the line.
  • ✓Keep people and pets away from sewage, and wash hands after any contact.
  • ✓Move belongings off the floor near the lowest drain.
  • ✓Find the sewer cleanout, usually a capped pipe in the basement, yard or crawl space, and clear access to it. The International Plumbing Code (IPC) calls for a cleanout where the building drain meets the building sewer, and it is the access point technicians use.
Prevention

Keeping the next clog away

Most repeat clogs trace to a few habits. Pour cooking grease into a can and not down the sink. Put only toilet paper in the toilet, because wipes labeled flushable do not break down like paper and are a frequent cause of main line blockages. Use a hair catcher in the tub.

Roots are the one cause habits cannot fix once they are in. Avoid planting trees or large shrubs over the sewer line, and have an older line inspected by camera if it has clogged more than once. Catching a root intrusion early means a cleaning and not a repair.

Slow drains turn up in every part of Windsor, from the older streets near Main Street to Water Valley and RainDance. If one drain is slow, the problem is usually local to that fixture, and when several back up at once the main line is the likelier cause.
