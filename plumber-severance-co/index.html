Who handles water and sewer in Severance

Severance gets its water from North Weld County Water District, which draws on the Colorado-Big Thompson project, and the Town lists water and sewer among its own utility services. It has been replacing aging water lines in the older part of town as the population grew.

Older parts of town and rural edges have historically used septic systems, according to a 1977 regional plan. Before a main line call, confirm whether the address is on the Town sewer or on septic, because the answer changes who to call and what to expect.

If it happens tonight

Before We Arrive: If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak in Severance

A few minutes of the right steps can keep a leak from becoming a flood. Use these while the 24/7 line gets a crew moving.

1

Close the main shutoff

The valve is usually where the water line enters the house. In an original-town home that tends to be on a basement wall, and in a newer build it is often in a mechanical closet by the heater. Turn it until the flow stops.

2

Kill power to wet areas

If water is touching outlets or the panel, stay out and cut power only from a dry spot. Otherwise switch off the breakers for the wet rooms.

3

Clear the area

Lift rugs, electronics and stored boxes off wet floors, and keep children and pets away if sewage is involved. Photograph the damage first for your insurer.

4

Call the 24/7 line

Call (970) 708-6013, give the street and the ZIP (80550, 80546, 80615 or 80610), and say what the water is doing. The line is answered around the clock.
