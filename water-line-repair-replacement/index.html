Symptoms

Wet ground, low pressure, or a bill that climbs with no leak inside

The pipe that brings water from the street to the house is buried, so its failures show up indirectly: a wet or sinking strip of lawn, pressure that fades to a trickle, a meter that turns while the house is dry. That pipe is the water service line.

Water line repair fixes a break in that pipe, and replacement installs a new line when the old one is failing or made of a material that should go. The line is also where the leak search from leak detection often ends.

Materials

Service line materials and what each means

A water line that fails inside the house is a supply pipe question for repiping, and a sudden break at the meter side is handled like burst pipe repair. Which of the two it is depends on where the failure sits relative to the meter.

Service line materials, their issues and the usual action
MaterialIssueUsual action
CopperDurable, but can pinhole or fail at jointsRepair a section or replace the run
Polyethylene (PE)Common in newer lines, resistant to freezing damageRepair with approved fittings
Galvanized steelCorrodes from the inside and restricts flowReplace the line
LeadHealth concern for drinking waterReplace, and check with your provider's inventory

Water providers are required to inventory service lines. The Town of Windsor's report says the inventory is available from Public Works at 970-674-5400, and Fort Collins-Loveland Water District answers the same questions at 970-226-3104.

Emergency

Emergency water line response in Windsor

A broken water line is an emergency when water is surfacing in the yard, the house has no water or the meter runs with every fixture off. Shut the main valve inside the house, call the 24/7 line and note where the ground is wet.

The leak may be on your side of the meter or on the provider's side, and the first visit works out which. Your side is repaired after the price is explained, and the provider's side is theirs to fix.

Low pressure

Low water pressure: where the problem usually sits

Low pressure at every fixture points to the line, a valve or the regulator, while one weak faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or cartridge. A gauge on a hose bib shows the number, and plumbing codes generally cap household pressure near 80 psi.

Common causes are a partly closed main valve, a failing pressure reducing valve, a corroded pipe or a leak in the service line. Rule out the leak first, because low pressure with a climbing water bill means water is escaping before it reaches the house.

Methods

Open trench or trenchless: how the two compare

Trenchless when

  • The route is straight and clear
  • Driveways, sidewalks or landscaping are in the way
  • The old line can serve as a pulling path
  • Minimal surface restoration matters

Open trench when

  • The line is badly damaged or collapsed
  • The route changes
  • Soil or utilities rule out a pull
  • A short run is cheaper to dig
Process

What a water line job involves

  1. 1

    Locate the line

    A line locator and the 811 call mark the path, and the leak is pinpointed.

  2. 2

    Choose the method

    Section repair, trenchless replacement or open trench is chosen from the footage and the route.

  3. 3

    Install

    New pipe is installed below the 30-inch frost line, with proper fittings and bedding. Pipe for drinking water must be approved for it, and the International Plumbing Code (IPC) points to NSF/ANSI 61.

  4. 4

    Test

    The line is pressure-tested before the trench is closed or the pipe is connected.

  5. 5

    Restore

    Surfaces, landscaping and irrigation lines are restored.

Windsor

Who owns what in Windsor

The Town operates the water system for Weld County addresses, and Fort Collins-Loveland Water District serves Larimer County addresses. In general, the provider owns the main and the meter, and the owner is responsible for the line from the meter to the house. Confirm the dividing point with your provider before assuming a leak is yours or theirs.

The Town's building code sets the frost line at 30 inches, and a new line should sit below it. The Town also requires a building permit to install or move plumbing, so ask what a service line replacement needs before work is scheduled.

Pricing

What affects the price of a water line job

Length, depth and surface drive the price. A short repair on an open lawn is a small job, and a long run under a driveway is a large one. The price is explained before work starts.
