Frozen Pipe Repair in Windsor, CO

No water at the faucet after a cold night, or a pipe that bulges or frosts over? Pipes are thawed safely, splits are repaired, and the weak spot is fixed so it does not repeat.

Symptoms

No water, a bulging pipe, or frost on a pipe

A faucet that gives a trickle or nothing on a cold morning, a frosted pipe in a garage or crawl space, or a bulge in a line are the signs. A frozen pipe is not always a split pipe, and a thawed pipe can start spraying as the ice melts, so the main shutoff should be within reach during a thaw.

Frozen pipe repair covers thawing the ice safely, finding any split, replacing the damaged section, and fixing the exposure that caused the freeze. A pipe that has already let go is treated as burst pipe repair.

The International Plumbing Code (IPC) bars water pipes from exterior walls, attics and crawl spaces unless they are protected from freezing, so a pipe that freezes there is often a protection problem as well as a weather problem. Irrigation lines are often managed by an HOA or metro district in newer subdivisions, so check who owns and winterizes yours.

Process

How a frozen pipe is thawed safely

  1. 1

    Find the freeze

    Open the faucet and trace the pipe to find the cold, firm section.

  2. 2

    Apply gentle heat

    A hair dryer, a heat lamp or warm towels thaw the pipe from the faucet end toward the frozen section.

  3. 3

    Keep the faucet open

    Running water as the ice melts relieves pressure and shows when flow returns.

  4. 4

    Check for splits

    Once flow returns, every pipe in the area is inspected for cracks, leaks and bulges.

  5. 5

    Fix the exposure

    Insulation, air sealing or rerouting follows, so the pipe is not left to freeze again.

Never use an open flame, a propane torch or a heat gun on a pipe. They can damage the pipe or start a fire.

Windsor

Why Windsor freezes are sudden

Windsor's building code puts the frost line at 30 inches, so the pipes that freeze are the ones above it, not the buried mains. Weather in the area swings fast, with Chinook winds that can raise January temperatures near 70 degrees and then a drop that takes pipes below freezing in hours. A line that held through a mild week can still freeze in the first hard night after it.

The practical answer is early preparation, not midwinter fixes. Disconnect hoses in the fall, keep garage doors closed on cold nights, open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls, and know where the main shutoff is. Splits found after a freeze are often followed by drying, covered under water damage restoration.

Safety

What to do if a pipe has already frozen

  • ✓Close the main shutoff before the ice melts, so a split cannot flood the house.
  • ✓Open the faucet on the frozen line so melting water can leave.
  • ✓Use warm air only. Never use an open flame, a torch or a heat gun.
  • ✓Keep cords and space heaters away from wet floors and pipes.
  • ✓If the frozen run is inside a closed wall, stop and call.
