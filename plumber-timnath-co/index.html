Before you call

If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak in Timnath, Do This First

Timnath's newer homes tend to hide their shutoffs in mechanical rooms and garages. Find yours now and these steps go faster on the day it matters.

1

Find the main shutoff

In a newer Timnath home it is often in the mechanical room or garage near the water heater. Turn it until the water stops, then open one faucet low in the house to relieve pressure.

2

Cut power near water

Switch off the breakers for any wet room, and for the water heater if the tank is leaking. If water is reaching the panel or outlets, stay out and wait for us.

3

Clear the area

Move furniture, rugs and electronics out of the spreading water. If the source is an irrigation or hose line, close it at its own valve.

4

Call the 24/7 line

Call (970) 708-6013 with the subdivision or street and the ZIP, 80547, 80525 or 80528. The line is answered around the clock, and the price is explained before we start.

Newer homes

What to check in a Timnath home that is under ten years old

Most Timnath homes are newer than the 2020 census, so the useful checks are about setup and not wear. Ask whether the water heater has a drain pan and an expansion tank, whether the main shutoff turns freely, and whether the outdoor hose bibs are the frost-proof kind. Look at the irrigation backflow device for a current test tag, since many providers require a yearly test and yours can say whether it does.

Water pressure deserves a gauge reading. The International Plumbing Code (IPC) caps static pressure at 80 psi, and high pressure shortens the life of fixtures and water heaters. Johnstown and Severance have a similar mix of newer builds. Calls from 80547, 80525 and 80528, and from nearby 80550 and 80538, reach the same dispatcher, with tankless service, leak detection and water heater repair as the first jobs to ask about.
